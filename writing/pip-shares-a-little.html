Picture-book manuscript · ages 3–5

Pip Shares a Little

Length: ~640 wordsSpreads: 12Theme: kindness & sharingRefrain: "Share a little, smile a lot!"
Blurb: Pip the hedgehog has just one red crayon, one turn on the swing and one small umbrella, and every time he shares, the day gets a little brighter. A playful read-aloud about how kindness grows when we pass it on.
Spread 1

Pip was a small hedgehog with a small backpack and a very big smile.

Every morning he walked to Sunny Hill Playgroup. Hop, hop, hop.

Art: Pip waving at the gate, autumn leaves, friends arriving.
Spread 2

Inside his backpack Pip had one red crayon.

Just one. Red as a berry. Red as a fire truck. Red as the very best apple.

Art: close-up of the crayon held up like treasure.
Spread 3

At the drawing table sat Mo the mole. Mo had paper. Mo had ideas.

But Mo had no crayon at all.

Pip looked at his crayon. Pip looked at Mo.

Spread 4

"Share a little…" whispered Pip, and he broke a tiny piece of red for Mo.

"…smile a lot!" laughed Mo.

Together they drew a red balloon, a red kite and a red, red sun.

Art: two drawings side by side, both glowing red.
Spread 5

After snack it was swing time. Swoosh, swoosh, swoosh!

Pip got the swing first. Up to the clouds. Down to the grass.

Spread 6

But by the slide stood Lulu the rabbit, holding her ears very tight. She had been waiting a long, long time.

Pip slowed down. Swoosh… swoosh… stop.

Spread 7

"Share a little…" said Pip, and he hopped off. "Your turn, Lulu!"

"…smile a lot!" sang Lulu, swinging so high her ears flew like flags.

Art: Lulu mid-swing, Pip pushing gently, both grinning.
Spread 8

At home time, grey clouds rolled over Sunny Hill. Plip. Plop. Pitter-patter.

Pip opened his small yellow umbrella. Just big enough for one hedgehog.

Spread 9

Under the big oak, Grandpa Badger stood with no umbrella and a newspaper on his head. Drip, drip, drip.

Pip looked at his umbrella. Pip looked at Grandpa Badger.

Spread 10

"Share a little…" said Pip, holding the umbrella as high as he could. It only covered Grandpa Badger's nose. They both started to giggle.

"…smile a lot!" chuckled Grandpa Badger.

Spread 11

Then, splash! Pip stepped in a puddle and his backpack tumbled into the mud. Pip's smile went small.

But here came Mo with a towel. Here came Lulu with a hug. Here came Grandpa Badger with a great big umbrella.

Art: friends arriving from all sides, the rain softening.
Spread 12

"Share a little…" they all said, squeezing together under one great big umbrella.

And Pip, warm and dry and happy, said the very best part:

"…SMILE A LOT!"

Art: everyone under the umbrella, a rainbow starting behind them.

Read-aloud notes

Pause before each "smile a lot!" so children can shout it. The three "Pip looked at… Pip looked at…" beats build anticipation; slow down there. Sound words (hop, swoosh, plip-plop) invite actions.
