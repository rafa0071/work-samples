Interactive murder mystery · case file

The Lighthouse Ledger

Players: 1–6Play time: 60–90 minDifficulty: mediumFormat: case file + 9 evidence items

The case

On a stormy night on Gull Island, retired harbour master Edmund Shaw is found at the bottom of the lighthouse stairs. The island's only ferry stopped at 6 p.m.; whoever did it is still on the island. The police call it a fall. His granddaughter asks you to look again, because the ledger he never let out of his sight is missing.

Suspects

Marta LlewellynInnkeeper, 52

Owed Edmund money for the inn's new roof. Says she was serving dinner until 10.

Callum ShawNephew, 31

Inherits the lighthouse. Arrived "on the last ferry" and claims he never left his room.

Dr. Priya NairMarine biologist, 44

Fought Edmund over a fishing-ban report. Was "checking her tide sensors" at the pier.

Owen PryceFerry captain, 60

Edmund's oldest friend. Says the storm kept him on the island for the night.

Evidence (excerpt)

#ItemWhat players learn
1Ferry timetableThe last ferry left the mainland at 5:15 p.m., not 6 p.m., on storm days.
2Inn dinner receiptsMarta rang up desserts at 9:12, 9:40 and 10:05 p.m.
3Tide sensor logPriya's sensor shows a manual reset at 9:31 p.m. at the pier.
4Lighthouse lamp logThe lamp was switched off for 7 minutes at 9:48 p.m.
5Callum's ferry ticketStamped with the 4:00 p.m. sailing, two hours earlier than he claims.
6Page torn from the ledgerLists "C.S. — £18,000 — 3rd warning".

Player questions

1. Who killed Edmund Shaw? 2. How did they get into the lighthouse unseen? 3. Why was the lamp switched off?

Solution (hidden from players)

Callum Shaw. He came on the 4 p.m. ferry (ticket, evidence 5), not the last one, giving him time to hide in the lighthouse store room. The ledger page shows his gambling debts to his uncle, who had given him a "third warning". At 9:48 p.m. Callum switched off the lamp so Owen, watching from the harbour, would not see two figures on the stairs. Red herrings: Marta (receipts give her an alibi) and Priya (the sensor reset is real but at the pier, a 20-minute walk away).

Each case is delivered as a printable/digital case file, character sheets, evidence cards and a host guide with a fair-play solution that players can reach from the clues alone.
