English (translation)

Cerro Azul · Huila, Colombia

Grown at 6,070 ft (1,850 m) by family coffee farmers in southern Huila. Hand-picked cherry by cherry and sun-dried on raised covered beds.

Tasting notes: brown sugar, tangerine and cocoa, with a bright acidity and a silky body.

VarietyCaturra & Castillo
ProcessWashed
RoastMedium
Size12 oz (340 g) valve bag

How to brew: 15 g of coffee per 8.5 oz (250 ml) of water at 198 °F (92 °C). Great for pour-over or French press.
