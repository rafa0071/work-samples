English (source)

Slow food, warm tables.

Seasonal plates from the coasts of Greece, Italy and Spain, cooked over wood fire and shared the way they're meant to be.

Reserve a table
Tables for up to 8 guests can be booked online. For larger groups or private dining, write to us and we'll plan it together.

Opening hours
Tue – Thu 5:30 – 10:30 pm · Fri – Sat noon – 11:30 pm · Sunday noon – 9 pm · Closed Mondays

Allergies
Let us know about any allergies when you book. Dishes marked GF are gluten-free; V means vegetarian.

Button: Book a table · Form error: Enter a valid email

Español (traducción)

Cocina sin prisa, mesas con calor.

Platos de temporada de las costas de Grecia, Italia y España, cocinados a la leña y pensados para compartir, como debe ser.

Reserva tu mesa
Puedes reservar en línea mesas de hasta 8 personas. Para grupos más grandes o eventos privados, escríbenos y lo organizamos juntos.

Horario
Mar – jue 17:30 – 22:30 · Vie – sáb 12:00 – 23:30 · Domingo 12:00 – 21:00 · Lunes cerrado

Alergias
Cuéntanos sobre cualquier alergia al hacer tu reserva. Los platos marcados con SG son sin gluten y V significa vegetariano.

Botón: Reservar mesa · Error del formulario: Ingresa un correo válido

Translator notes

DecisionWhy
"Slow food, warm tables" → "Cocina sin prisa, mesas con calor"A literal "comida lenta" sounds negative in Spanish; the rhythm of the tagline is kept instead.
12-hour times → 24-hour formatStandard for restaurant hours in most Spanish-speaking countries.
GF → SG (sin gluten)Dietary labels localized so menu badges stay meaningful.
"tú" formMatches the brand's friendly tone; can switch to "usted" for a formal market.
